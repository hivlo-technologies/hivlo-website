    The journey

    One child's journey, one flow.

    From the first day to going home, each step lives in the same record and hands over to the next.

    1. AdmissionIntake, placement and orientation
    2. IDPThe plan for the child, with review dates
    3. Care and learningEducation, programmes, family contact
    4. WellbeingMedical, therapy, behaviour and incidents
    5. ReviewMDT meetings, court orders, reports
    6. ReunificationGoing home, with the story intact
    What HIVLO CMP does

    Plain screens, plain words, and a record that stays honest.

    Designed with the staff who use it every day.

    One record per child

    Everything about a child sits in one record: personal details, documents, family, learning, wellbeing and history. Enter it once, see it everywhere it is needed.

    Traffic-light summary

    Each child's summary shows green, amber or red per area, so staff go straight to what is outstanding or urgent.

    Case management

    Admissions, IDP, court orders, case notes, visits, consents, movements and MDT meetings, with reviews and due dates that do not get lost.

    Family and placement

    Family contacts, host parents, genogram, out-weekends and reunification, kept with the child's record.

    Learning and wellbeing

    Education, extra-murals, medical, therapy, behaviour and incidents, with school report import.

    Honest records

    Nothing is deleted, only voided with a reason. Every change records who and when, and prints are logged too.

    Reports and Excel

    Ready reports and printouts, plus a built-in Report Builder with a Child assistant that drafts MDT, panel and DSD reports from the record, with the source of every fact shown.

    Inventory

    Dry goods stock with the value of every item, food parcels, stock takes and forecasts, built in.

    Emergency Medical Pack

    One tap from any page gives the medical details a responder needs.

    Safe by design

    Role-based access, backups and restore, signed updates with automatic roll-back, and a licence tied to your own server.

    MAYA, your assistant

    Ask how to do something in your own words, or ask about a child's IDP review, court order or medical follow-ups, and get an answer with a link to the right screen. Switched on by your administrator for each person.

    Ask MAYA

    Ask in your own words.

    MAYA is the built-in assistant. Ask how to do something and get the steps with a link to the right screen, or ask about a child's IDP review, court order or medical follow-ups. It is switched on by your administrator for each person, and the words you type are not stored.

    Privacy

    Your information stays yours.

    HIVLO CMP runs on a server dedicated to your centre, never shared with other centres. This website only carries product information. No child information is ever sent to it. Updates are digitally signed, so HIVLO CMP refuses any file that did not come from the official supplier.
