What we stand for

Care work is about children, not paperwork. Everything we build is meant to give staff their time back, without ever putting a child's information at risk.

Honest records

Nothing is deleted, only voided with a reason, and every change records who and when.

4

Safe to update

HIVLO installs updates for you. A backup is taken first, so your records are protected.
