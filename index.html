Why HIVLO

Named for the hive. Built to flow.

HIVLO comes from hive flow. A bee hive is one of the most organised and efficient places in nature. Thousands of bees, each with a part to play, work as one. Nothing is wasted, nothing is repeated and nothing is lost.

A children's home should run the same way. People, information and processes move together, and everyone knows their part, so staff can spend their time on the children.

Everyone has a part

In a hive every bee has a job. In HIVLO every person signs in with their own role and sees what that role allows, so each person does their part in one place.

Nothing is wasted

The honeycomb is made of hexagons because they hold the most in the least space, with no gaps. HIVLO works the same way: enter it once, and it shows wherever it is needed. Nothing is typed twice.

Nothing is lost

A hive keeps everything it has gathered. HIVLO keeps every record: nothing is deleted, only voided with a reason, and every change records who and when.

What we stand for

Care work is about children, not paperwork.

Everything we build is meant to give staff their time back, without ever putting a child's information at risk.

Simple to use

Plain screens and plain words, with Help that understands questions asked in your own words.

Your data stays yours

HIVLO CMP runs on a server dedicated to your centre. This website never holds a child's information.

Honest records

Nothing is deleted, only voided with a reason, and every change records who and when.

Safe to update

HIVLO installs updates for you. A backup is taken first, so your records are protected.

See it

Every child, at a glance.

Each child's summary shows green, amber or red per area, so staff go straight to what is outstanding or urgent. Ask MAYA in your own words and get the steps, with a link to the right screen.
