What we stand for

Care work is about children, not paperwork.

Everything we build is meant to give staff their time back, without ever putting a child's information at risk.

Simple to use

Plain screens and plain words, with Help that understands questions asked in your own words.

Your data stays yours

HIVLO CMP runs on a server dedicated to your centre. This website never holds a child's information.

Honest records

Nothing is deleted, only voided with a reason, and every change records who and when.

Safe to update

HIVLO installs updates for you. A backup is taken first, so your records are protected.

See it

Every child, at a glance.

Each child's summary shows green, amber or red per area, so staff go straight to what is outstanding or urgent. Ask MAYA in your own words and get the steps, with a link to the right screen.
