Security

Built so a child's information stays protected.

HIVLO CMP is designed around one idea: the records of the children in your care belong to you, and they stay on your own server.

How HIVLO protects information

Plain answers, with nothing claimed that we cannot stand behind.

1

Your data stays with you

HIVLO CMP runs on a server at your centre, on your own network. There is no HIVLO cloud service, and this website never receives a child's information.

2

The right people see the right things

Every person signs in with their own account and a role. Health information is shown only to people who hold the Medical privilege, and drug testing is never included in drafted reports.

3

A record you can trust

Nothing is deleted, only voided with a reason. Every change records who and when, and printing is logged too, so you can see what happened and who did it.

4

Backups and restore

Backups are taken from inside HIVLO, and a backup is taken before an update is installed. Administrators can restore from a backup if it is ever needed.

5

Signed updates

Updates are digitally signed. HIVLO refuses an update that did not come from the official supplier, and our technicians install updates for you.

6

AI is optional and stays local

The Assistant works without any AI model. If your administrator chooses to switch on optional wording help, it runs only on your own network, never in someone else's cloud, and names are replaced before any text reaches it. It is off by default.

POPIA and your responsibilities

HIVLO CMP is designed to support a centre in meeting its POPIA obligations, for example by keeping information in one controlled place, limiting who can see it and recording who changed what. Meeting POPIA also depends on how your centre uses and manages the system. HIVLO does not hold certifications we have not been awarded, and we will not claim them.

What we ask of you

Keep your server somewhere safe, give each person their own sign-in, and never email a child's name or case details to us. If you need help, describe the problem, and we will look at it with you.

Questions about security?

Ask us anything about how HIVLO CMP keeps information safe. We are happy to explain it to you or your management.
