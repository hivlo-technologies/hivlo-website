Support

We look after your HIVLO from start to finish.

Our technicians build, install, set up and train. When you need help, you write to us and we help you.

How to get help

1

Email support

Customers write to support@hivlo.co.za. This is the way to ask for help, report a problem or request a change.

2

Built-in Help

Press the yellow hexagon on any HIVLO page and ask your question in your own words. It shows the steps and a link to the right screen.

3

Your administrator

Your own HIVLO administrator can answer most day-to-day questions, such as passwords and who can see what.

What to put in a support email

  • The name of your centre and who you are
  • What you were trying to do and what happened instead
  • The build number, shown in HIVLO under Administration, Updates
  • A screenshot of the problem, with any child's name or details hidden

What never to send

Please never email a child's name, reference number or case details to us. We do not need them to help you, and email is not a safe place for them. If we need to see something, we will tell you how to show it to us safely.

New to HIVLO?

Installation, set-up, administration and staff training are done by HIVLO technicians, so there is nothing for you to download or install yourself. Interested in HIVLO CMP? Write to info@hivlo.co.za.
