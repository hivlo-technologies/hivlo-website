About HIVLO

Built from real child and youth care work.

HIVLO Technologies builds software for child and youth care centres, so staff spend less time on paperwork and more time with the children.

Why HIVLO exists

HIVLO began as a workbook built for a children's home. It grew into a full case-management system, shaped by the staff who use it every day. Care work is about children, not paperwork, and everything we build is meant to give people their time back without ever putting a child's information at risk.

Frequently asked questions

Do we need the internet?

Your team uses HIVLO on your own network, from a web browser. Our technicians may need internet access when they install or update the system.

What hardware do we need?

One Windows computer acts as the server. Every other computer uses Edge or Chrome to reach it. We help you choose the right machine.

Who owns our data?

You do. It is stored on your server at your centre, and HIVLO has no copy of it.

Can it replace paper files and spreadsheets?

Yes. That is what it was built for: one record for every child, with the family, learning, health and case history kept together.

Who installs it and trains our staff?

HIVLO technicians build the server, install the system, set it up, administer it and train your staff. You do not install anything yourself.

How are updates done?

HIVLO installs updates for you when they are ready. A backup is taken first so your records are protected.

What language is it in?

English.

How many people can use it?

One licence covers your server, with any number of users and computers.
