About HIVLO

Built from real child and youth care work.

HIVLO Solutions builds software for child and youth care centres, so staff spend less time on paperwork and more time with the children.

Why HIVLO exists

From a workbook to a full system.

HIVLO began as a workbook built for a children's home. It grew into a full case-management system, shaped by the staff who use it every day. Care work is about children, not paperwork, and everything we build is meant to give people their time back without ever putting a child's information at risk.

What we stand for

Three promises.

Simple to use

Plain screens and plain words, with Help that understands questions asked in your own words.

Your data stays yours

HIVLO CMP runs on a server dedicated to your centre, never shared with other centres. This website never holds a child's information.

Honest records

Nothing is deleted, only voided with a reason, and every change records who and when.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Do we need the internet?

Your team uses HIVLO from a web browser. How the server is set up is agreed with you, and our technicians take care of it.

What hardware do we need?

HIVLO CMP runs on a server dedicated to your centre. We work out with you what is needed and set it all up. Your team then uses Edge or Chrome on their own computers.

Who owns our data?

You do. It is stored on the server dedicated to your centre and is never shared with other centres.

Can it replace paper files and spreadsheets?

Yes. That is what it was built for: one record for every child, with the family, learning, health and case history kept together.

Who installs it and trains our staff?

HIVLO technicians build the server, install the system, set it up, administer it and train your staff. You do not install anything yourself.

How are updates done?

HIVLO installs updates for you when they are ready. A backup is taken first so your records are protected.

What language is it in?

English.

How many people can use it?

One licence covers your server, with any number of users and computers.

How do we ask for a quote or demonstration?

Write to info@hivlo.co.za and tell us about your centre.
