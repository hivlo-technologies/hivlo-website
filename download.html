Products

HIVLO CMP is a licensed product made up of a core system and optional packs. Pricing is on request.

1

HIVLO CMP (core product)

The case-management system for a child and youth care centre: children, case management, family, learning, wellbeing, calendar, reports and administration. Required for the packs. One licence covers your server, with any number of users and PCs.

2

Inventory pack

Stationery and stock control, added to the core product.

3

Assistant and Report Builder pack

Builds MDT packs, panel reports and DSD progress reports, and answers questions about a child in plain words. Optional AI wording help is off by default and runs locally only.

How we work

HIVLO technicians build your server, install the system, set it up, administer it and train your staff. There is nothing for you to download or install yourself, and updates are installed by HIVLO as needed.
