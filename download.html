Why it matters

What changes when everything is in one place.

Care staff already work hard. HIVLO takes the admin off their plate and keeps the record honest.

Nothing falls through the cracks

Court orders, IDP reviews, medical follow-ups and meetings carry due dates and show up as green, amber or red, so the urgent work is always visible.

Report time drops

MDT packs, panel reports and DSD progress reports start as a drafted first version built from the child's own record, with the source of every fact shown.

Ready for audits and inspections

Nothing is deleted, only voided with a reason. Every change records who and when, so your records can be trusted.

Children's information stays private

It lives on a server dedicated to your centre, never shared with other centres. Each person sees only what their role allows.

What is included

One product, everything built in.

HIVLO CMP, the Centre Management Platform, is one product. Inventory, the Report Builder and the MAYA assistant are part of it. One licence covers your server, with any number of users and PCs.

Built in

Case management

The case-management heart of the Centre Management Platform, for a child and youth care centre.

  • One record per child: details, documents, family, learning, wellbeing and history
  • Admission, IDP, court orders, case notes, visits, consents, movements and MDT meetings
  • Traffic-light summary for every child
  • Family contacts, host parents, genogram, out-weekends and reunification
  • Education, medical, therapy, behaviour and incident records, with school report import
  • Calendar, staff, ready-made reports and Excel export
  • Built-in Help that answers questions asked in your own words
Built in

Inventory

Know what you have, what it is worth and what you will need.

  • Dry goods stock with the value of every item, purchased or donated
  • Food parcels in set sizes, with packing checklists and printed parcels
  • Stock takes: count, upload, review the differences, confirm
  • Forecasts so you buy ahead, not in a panic
  • Reports on spend, donations and parcels given out
Built in

Report Builder and MAYA

Get the answers and documents you need, without the typing.

  • Build your own reports, with charts, anonymised output, printing and Excel, and 16 ready-made reports
  • Child assistant: a first draft of an MDT pack, panel report or DSD progress report, with where each fact came from
  • MAYA, the built-in assistant: ask how to do something in your own words and get the steps with a link to the right screen
  • On a child's record, ask MAYA about the IDP review, court order or medical follow-ups, and get a straight answer
  • A management overview and proposed reminders for overdue items, which you confirm
  • MAYA is switched on by your administrator for each person, and the words you type are not stored
  • Optional AI wording help is off until a HIVLO technician sets it up, and runs only on your own dedicated server, never in a public AI service

Licensing

How licensing works.

Simple, and fair for centres of any size. Pricing is shared with your quote.

One licence per server

Your licence covers your server, with any number of users and computers. No per-person fees.

One key, valid for a year

Your licence is a key that lists your products and an expiry date. It is checked on your own server, with no internet needed.

Add-ons are added to the key

When you add an add-on, we issue an updated key that lists it. All your products share one expiry date.

One key to renew

Renewal is a single new key for everything. Your records are never locked away: they can still be viewed, printed and exported.
