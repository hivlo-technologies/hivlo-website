Core product

HIVLO CMP

The complete case-management system for a child and youth care centre.

How the packs plug in

Packs are expansions to the core. They share the core's sign-in, privileges and audit trail, so staff learn one system, not several.

HIVLO CMP core

Switch-on rules: an expansion works only when it is installed and your licence includes it. It is updated together with the core, and if it is not licensed it simply stays hidden. Nothing is ever deleted.

How licensing works

Simple, and fair for centres of any size. Pricing is shared with your quote.

1

One licence per server

Your licence covers your server, with any number of users and computers. No per-person fees.

2

One key, valid for a year

Your licence is a key that lists your products and an expiry date. It is checked on your own server, with no internet needed.

3

Packs are added to the key

When you add a pack, we issue an updated key that lists it. All your products share one expiry date.

4

One key to renew

Renewal is a single new key for everything. Your records are never locked away: they can still be viewed, printed and exported.
